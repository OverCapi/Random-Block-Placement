# Changelog

## 0.4.0

### Added
- **Quick preset wheel**: hold `R` to open a radial wheel with 9 preset slots. Point at a slice and release to load that preset and turn placement on. Right-click a slice to assign a preset to it. Assignments are saved in the config.
- **Preset sharing**: send a preset or the current selection to chosen online players with `/msg`, or post it in public chat. Players who have the mod get an `[Import]` button in chat.
- **Clipboard sharing**: copy a preset as a code, then import it from the Presets tab with "Import from clipboard".

- New mod logo.
